WHAT THIS MEASURES
------------------
For each topic, TF-IDF cosine similarity is computed between every pair
of prompts WITHIN that topic (not across topics). Similarity ranges from
0 (completely different wording) to 1 (identical). A pair flagged above
0.6 is considered a potential near-duplicate and worth reviewing.

The goal: confirm that the 85 new prompts don't just paraphrase each other,
so that scaling from 15 to 100 prompts/topic actually adds new signal for
the classifier.
